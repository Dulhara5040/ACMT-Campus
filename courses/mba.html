Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Offered by City University Malaysia

Program Overview

Master of Business Administration is an academically rigorous coursework programme that is innovative, practical and globally relevant.

Our MBA curriculum combines personalised leadership development with a strategised multidisciplinary approach to provide a broad understanding of theories with real world issues beyond the boundaries of textbooks.

Course Contents

  • 1. Marketing Management
  • 2. Accounting and Finance for Managers
  • 3. Managerial Economics
  • 4. International Business
  • 5. Managing People in Organizations
  • 6. Strategic Management – Asian Business
  • 7. Operations Management
  • 8. Supply Chain Management
  • 9. Entrepreneurship and Innovation
  • 10. Project Management
  • 11. Research Methods
