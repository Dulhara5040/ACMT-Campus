Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) (Honours)

Offered by City University Malaysia

Program Overview

The overall objectives of this academic Bachelor’s programmes is to develop the students’ intellectual capacity, executive personality, and managerial skills in a way that enables them to assume entry-level managerial positions in business and industry, public sector organisations, consultancy companies and other organisations.

Graduates of the programmes may also choose to start their own entrepreneurial business ventures. This programme is also designed to produce graduates who are skilled and job ready upon graduation, sensitive to their operating environment and to the factors that can enhances work effectiveness.

Course Contents

  • 1. English for Academic Purposes, Business Communication & English for Employment
  • 2. Principles of Finance ,Introduction Accountancy & Management Accounting
  • 3. Principles of Macroeconomics ,Entrepreneurship
  • 4. Management Information system
  • 5. Principles of Management, Introduction to Business
  • 6. Principles of Marketing & Ethics in Business
  • 7. Business Statistics & Business mathematics
  • 8. Research & Development ,Product Development
  • 9. Small Business Management & Organizational Behaviour
  • 10. Operation Management
  • 11. International Logistic & Transport , Supply Chain Managemen
  • 12. Financial Management
  • 13. Commercial Law
  • 14. International Human Resource Management
  • 15. Business Research Method
