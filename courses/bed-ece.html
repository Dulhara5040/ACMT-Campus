B.Ed.(Hons) Early Childhood Education

Offered by City University Malaysia

Program Overview

This Bachelor of Education (Honours) in Early Childhood Education is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to become effective educators and leaders in the field of early childhood education.

Course Contents

  • 1. Education Psychology & Philosophy of Education
  • 2. Introduction to Early Childhood Education & Children's Emotional and Social Development
  • 3. Child Growth & Development & Teacher's Ethics and Professionalism
  • 4. Instructional Technology and Learning & Pedagogical Models and Strategies
  • 5. Curriculum Development & Co-Curriculum Education
  • 6. Children With Special Needs & Guidance and Counselling in School
  • 7. English For Academic Purposes & Language and Literacy for Early Childhood Education
  • 8. Mathematics For Early Childhood Education & Science and Technology For Early Childhood Education
  • 9. Music, Movement and Play For Early Childhood Education & Assessment and Observation For Early Childhood Education
  • 10. Children's Art and Creativity & Academic Skills
  • 11. Family Involvement in Early Childhood Education & Spiritual & Moral Development Of Young Children
  • 12. Introduction To E-Learning & English For Employment
  • 13. Children’s Health, Nutrition and Safety
  • 14. Communities and Culture & Management and Quality Early Childhood Education Physical
  • 15. Entrepreneurship
  • 16. Early Childhood Education Curriculum & Research in The Classroom
